# 2012 Historical Repository — Source Notes

The first loaded batch consists of official 2012 budget/project records from:
Ministry of Finance, Guyana — Estimates of the Public Sector, Current and Capital Revenue and Expenditure, 2012, Volume 1.

Important:
- These are budgeted projects/programmes, not automatically contract awards.
- Contractor/Awardee remains null unless a procurement/award source confirms it.
- The initial batch includes selected records from the Office of the Prime Minister, Ministry of Finance and Ministry of Public Works.
- 2012 is marked Partial / Expanding until all relevant estimate volumes and complementary official sources are processed.
